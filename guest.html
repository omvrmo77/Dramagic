Our Story

The moment Dramagic became real

Dramagic was born when we saw learners who loved theatre but were afraid of going on stage. The first time they performed, something changed. They felt the lights, the audience, and the sound of people clapping for them. After that, they became more confident — and that feeling became our mission.
